# Privacy notice for 24Seven.FM Player

Last updated: October 3, 2026.

24Seven.FM Player is an unofficial, non-commercial native Android client for the five public 24Seven.FM radio stations. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by 24Seven.FM or its stations.

## Data the app handles

- **Radio and public station data:** The app connects directly to the selected station to play audio and retrieve public now-playing details, both while playing and while the app is open and not yet playing (including who requested the current track, an optional request message, the station's listener count, and the track length), queue, history (including the station's played-history archive for a date and time the user chooses), station news, artwork, catalog, chat, and the signed-in member's public favorite-track list. When the user chooses **View profile** on a member's name, the app asks the station for that member's public profile card (name, member-since date, location, rank, membership, badges, forum post count, online status, and the contact links the member's card lists) and shows it; the card is kept in memory only. When a signed-in user taps the **Public Favorites** badge on that card, the app asks the station for the favorite-track list that member has made public and shows it; that list is also kept in memory only. As with ordinary internet connections, the station, stream host, content-delivery network, and their network providers can receive connection information such as the source IP address under their own practices; the Player does not read or persist that address itself.
- **Account credentials:** A username, password, and station security code are sent only to the selected station when the user explicitly signs in. Passwords and security-code answers are held only long enough to complete that sign-in attempt and are not written to app storage.
- **Station sessions:** Successful station cookies and the returned display identity are encrypted locally using an Android Keystore key. Sessions are scoped to the exact station and can be removed with Sign out or by clearing the app's data.
- **Account boundary:** The Player signs into pre-existing station accounts; it does not create or delete them. **Sign out** removes only the protected session from this device. It does not delete the station account, public Chat posts, request history, station/server logs, or email already sent through another app. Station-side access, correction, retention, and deletion are controlled by the applicable station or network operator.
- **User submissions:** Chat posts, song requests, optional request messages, private messages, album ratings, changes to the member's own favorite tracks, and edits to the member's own station profile are sent only after an explicit user action. The app does not automate or bulk-submit them.
- **Private messages:** Where verified for a station, a signed-in member can open **Private messages** to read their station Inbox, Sent, and Saved folders, and to reply to or start a message. Folder lists and an opened message are fetched from the station only on request. So the app can show an unread-message count on the More tab, it also asks the station for that count when the member signs in, returns to the app, or moves between tabs, at most once every two minutes; this reads the Inbox list but opens no message. All of it is kept in memory, cleared from the interface on sign-out, and never written to app storage, notifications, diagnostics, or logs. Opening a message marks it as read on the station, as the station website does. A message is sent only after the member reviews it and taps **Send**; it is submitted once, a copy is kept in the station's Sent folder, and the draft is not saved by the app. The station stores private messages with the sender's and recipient's accounts under its own practices.
- **Favorites and album ratings from the Player:** Where verified for a station, a signed-in member can add the current track to their station favorites or rate the current album. Each is sent to the station only after an explicit tap (and, for a rating, after choosing a value and confirming). The result is kept in memory only. The station stores the favorite and the rating with the member's account. A signed-in member can also see their favorites in the order they rank them (the order the station shows on the member's own profile page, read 50 at a time from the station's profile favorites feed with the member's session), move a track up or down in that ranking, or remove a track from their favorites; each change is one request sent after choosing it from the track's menu (removal asks for confirmation), and the ranking (and after a removal, the full list) is then read again from the station.
- **Album reviews:** The reviews a station shows on an album's page are loaded, in memory only, when the album is opened. The Player does not write reviews; they are written on the station's own site.
- **Station community pages:** On request, the Player loads the station's public Recently Added albums, the Online Now block and members list (member names, ranks, locations and flags, join dates, post counts, and online or VIP marks as the station publishes them), and the station's events and birthdays calendar (theme days, composer birthdays, and member birthdays with the age the station prints). Member names and birthdays are shown only when community content is visible in the Player's settings. These pages are read with plain requests, kept in memory only, and never stored or sent anywhere else. A member-list search sends only the typed name part to the station.
- **Edit profile:** Where verified for a station, a signed-in member can open their own station profile form in the Player and change their real name, location, country, occupation, interests, website, signature, about text, newsletter choice, and whether their online status is hidden. The Player reads the station's whole form with the member's session, lets the member change only those fields, and sends the form back once, to the form's own address, when the member chooses Save; the password fields are always sent empty, so the password cannot change from the Player. Email, forum, and chat settings on that form are not editable in the Player and are returned unchanged. The form is then read again so the Player shows what the station kept. It stays in memory only.
- **Community safety and notification preferences:** The app stores the adult/not-adult result from its age screen, the accepted Terms version, the mature-community-content visibility choice, station-scoped blocked display identities, the stations for which the user enables local Chat-mention notifications, and (when chosen) one station identifier for the closed-app monitor. It does not save the date of birth entered on the age screen.
- **Local Chat-mention notifications and monitor:** When enabled, exact signed-in display-name mentions are matched on the device. The first snapshot is only a baseline, duplicate fingerprints are bounded in memory, blocked authors are filtered, and Android notification text contains the station and sender but not the Chat message. Separately, the user may opt into a visible Android foreground monitor for one signed-in station. It fetches that station's Chat about once a minute while active, shows a persistent notification with a Stop action, stops when sign-in or community consent is no longer valid, and does not forward station sessions or Chat data to a developer-operated service. It is polling, not server push, and can still be stopped by Android or the user.
- **Android backup and transfer:** All app-private data is excluded from Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer. Protected station sessions and local safety preferences are not migrated through those Android mechanisms.
- **Abuse reports:** After an explicit **Review email** action, the app prepares a bounded draft containing the selected station, report category, reported username, displayed timestamp, a bounded Chat, request, or private-message snapshot, reporter name or station nickname, and optional details. Android opens the user's chosen email app with the monitored moderation recipient, subject, and draft body prefilled. The user may review, edit, cancel, or explicitly send it there. The Player cannot read the user's email account or confirm whether the draft was sent or delivered, and it does not persist the draft or report. The selected email app and receiving administration may retain a sent report under their own practices.
- **App feedback:** After an explicit **Review email draft** action in **Report a problem**, the app prepares a local email draft with the selected category, selected station, preparation timestamp, and any optional description the user entered. A separate unchecked **Include privacy-safe diagnostics** control is required before the draft includes the local diagnostic snapshot. Android opens the user's email app addressed to the monitored Player contact; the user may review, edit, cancel, or explicitly send it there. The Player cannot read the email account, confirm sending or delivery, or persist the draft or feedback. The selected email app and recipient may retain a sent message under their own practices.
- **Chat history:** Chat messages are kept in memory for the current app session and are not persisted by the app.
- **Favorite tracks:** Favorite lists are loaded only after station sign-in, kept in memory, and cleared from the interface when the user signs out. They are not written to app storage.
- **Request activity and membership:** Where verified for a station, the app can load the signed-in member's recent requests, station-reported request readiness, the station's estimate of when a queued request will play, and the member's membership and rank as shown on their public profile card. These values are kept in memory, cleared from the interface on sign-out, and are not written to app storage.
- **External contact and station pages:** The current Player exposes only **Contact Us**, which opens an Android email composer addressed to the monitored Player contact after an explicit tap; the app does not send the message itself. A member's profile card can also show two links from the station's own card: **Email**, which opens the station's email-a-member page in the browser, and the member's listed website. Each opens in the browser only after an explicit tap; the Player sends nothing to either and does not share its station session with the browser. VIP/RIP membership, payment, registration, recovery, management, and deletion pages are not linked from the Player. The app does not copy its protected station session into the email app. Drafts, sent mail, and retention are controlled independently by the selected email app and recipient.
- **In-app diagnostics:** The app can generate a local support snapshot containing app/build version, Android/API and coarse device model, selected station, bounded playback/error category, the most recent in-memory stream-start or station-switch duration, validated-network availability, broad audio-output category, and up to five recent non-sensitive playback transitions. The preview excludes account data, messages, request/report content, URLs, route names, stable device identifiers, raw errors, and logs. It remains on the device unless the user explicitly copies it to Android's clipboard or opens Android's Share chooser and selects a recipient; the receiving app then handles the text under its own privacy practices.

The application does not include advertising, crash reporting, tracking SDKs, developer-operated analytics, or a developer-operated data server. The Google Cast Android Sender SDK automatically sends encrypted, anonymous Cast-interaction diagnostics to a Google log-collection server. Google documents these diagnostics as generic discovery and session-management events, mobile-device information, and client Cast-app information used in aggregate to monitor Cast SDK use and performance and detect product defects. Google states that these logs do not contain identifiers traceable to a specific user, are retained briefly before aggregation, are not transferred to third parties or other apps, and cannot be opted out of or deleted by the app developer or user.

An authorized network Administrator states that all five stations share one policy: account username, email address, and IP address support account security and network access; cookies support signed-in sessions, preferences, and Chat; and non-identifying browser, operating-system, and page-view data may be collected. Station-side retention periods are stated under Retention and deletion. The stated access roles are the station owner and network administrators. Information is not sold, traded, or rented, and is not shared with another provider except PayPal when processing premium subscriptions or donations; financial data is not stored directly on station servers. Station operators and network providers may independently retain normal server or network logs; those systems are outside this application's control.

## Android permissions

- **Internet:** Required for live streams and station features.
- **Network state:** Used by the media/network libraries to detect connectivity changes and present connection or retry states.
- **Foreground media playback:** Required to continue audio while the app is in the background and to expose system media controls.
- **Foreground special-use service:** Required only for the optional, user-started one-station Chat-mention monitor. Android keeps it visibly disclosed through a persistent notification while active.
- **Notifications:** On Android 13 and newer, requested contextually for visible media-playback controls and optional local Chat-mention alerts. Denying it does not prevent public playback or Chat use, but may remove those notification surfaces.
- **Wake lock:** Used only by the media playback stack to keep an active live stream from being suspended while the screen is off. The app does not use it to keep the display awake.

Android also adds an app-specific, signature-protected dynamic-receiver permission during the build. It is an internal platform safety mechanism that prevents other apps from sending to non-exported receivers; users are not asked to grant it and it does not provide access to user data.

The app does not request contacts, location, microphone, camera, photos, phone, SMS, or broad file-storage access.

## Retention and deletion

Protected station sessions, the adult age-screen result, accepted Terms version, community-content visibility preference, local block list, station-scoped Chat-mention opt-ins, and the optional foreground-monitor station remain on the originating device until the relevant in-app action, app-data clearing, or uninstall removes them. They are excluded from Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer. Chat, mention-deduplication fingerprints, favorite-track lists, request activity, membership indicators, private-message lists, opened private messages and unsent private-message drafts, album-rating choices, pending song-request text, abuse-report and feedback draft data, and generated diagnostic previews are transient. A report, feedback, or Contact Us draft handed to an email app is then controlled by that app and, if sent, by the recipient. A copied diagnostic snapshot is controlled by Android's clipboard, and a shared snapshot is controlled by the recipient selected by the user. Android's app settings can clear all locally retained application data.

To request access to, correction of, or deletion of station-side data, email [morg@24seven.fm](mailto:morg@24seven.fm) with the station name, the account name, and the request type, or use the applicable station's Contact/Feedback system. The administrator verifies the request before acting. Do not include a password, security-code answer, session value, or other authentication secret. This request path covers account information, sign-in/session and IP/security logs, station-library searches, public Chat posts, song requests and optional request messages, private messages, album ratings, and abuse-report information that the station retains. The five stations share one policy: the station owner and network administrators may access the information, and it is not shared with another provider except PayPal when processing premium subscriptions or donations. Clearing local app data does not remove station-side accounts, content, or logs.

For verified deletion requests, 24seven.FM deletes or anonymizes personally identifiable account and profile data from active systems within 30 days. Favorites and account preferences are deleted with the account within the same period. Ordinary song-request records, operational logs, Chat and forum data, and normal diagnostics/server/IP logs are kept for no more than 90 days. The station has not stated a separate retention period for private messages or album ratings; the request path above applies to them. Chat or forum content may be anonymized rather than removed where necessary to preserve conversation continuity or moderation context, but direct identifiers are unlinked. A record may be retained only while an active security, abuse, fraud, or rule-enforcement investigation requires it, and is deleted when no longer required. Lawful legal or regulatory retention obligations can require a longer period.

Encrypted backups are retained for no more than 90 days. Deleted data is not intentionally restored; if disaster recovery restores deleted information, the deletion is reapplied. Payment providers retain payment credentials and any legally required transaction records for their own required period; 24seven.FM does not directly store payment-card information. Irreversibly aggregated or anonymized statistics may be retained indefinitely because they no longer identify an individual.

## Reporting problems

When reporting a problem, do not include credentials, private messages, session values, or security-code images. Closed-test participants should use a non-administrator station account where practical.

The application is maintained by the community contributors to the `James-Jennison/24Seven.FM-Player` project. Privacy questions about the Player can be sent to [24sevenplayer@jamesjennison.net](mailto:24sevenplayer@jamesjennison.net). For station-side access, correction, or deletion requests, contact [morg@24seven.fm](mailto:morg@24seven.fm), the network-authorized contact for this application. Reports can also be submitted through [the project's GitHub issue channel](https://github.com/James-Jennison/24Seven.FM-Player/issues) without sensitive account information.

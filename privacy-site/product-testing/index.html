Review your application

Review the details above with Back before sending. We use this profile only to match you with suitable testing work.

A validated application is recorded privately in the project’s protected Tester Hub for closed-test review and assignment, then sent privately to the project coordinator. It is not published and does not grant Google Play access or acceptance. Guest testing does not require a station account. Existing access means only whether you already have an account; never create an account or submit passwords, security answers, CAPTCHA answers, or other login information. Testing preferences help us match focused assignments; use the Player support route to withdraw or request deletion.

What happens next

Access, then a focused assignment

  1. Follow the access instructions supplied by your recruitment source.
  2. Register the device coverage and Google-account email you will use for testing.
  3. Complete Google Play opt-in only after you are eligible, then use the Tester Hub to receive and report focused assignments.

Please do not submit: station usernames or passwords, CAPTCHA answers, screenshots with personal information, or private messages. You need your own Google account only to opt in to Google Play; guest testing does not require a station account.

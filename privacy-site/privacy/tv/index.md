## Privacy notice for 24Seven.FM TV

Last updated: August 24, 2026.

24Seven.FM TV is a native Android TV listener application for the five public
24Seven.FM stations. Its current alpha is an anonymous, listener-first
experience.

## Data handled

- The selected station receives the normal network connection information
  required to play its public live stream and retrieve public Now Playing,
  artwork, Queue, and Recently Played data under that station's own practices.
  The app does not collect or persist an IP address.
- The app stores the last-selected station and a local sleep-timer preference on
  the device so it can restore the listener experience. No account, member,
  chat, request, or payment data is collected or exposed in the TV alpha.
- The optional local Device status view contains only app/build information,
  coarse device and Android details, the selected station, bounded
  playback/network/audio state, and a short non-sensitive transition history.
  It excludes account data, messages, URLs, identifiers, raw errors, and logs.

The app contains no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting, tracking SDKs,
microphone access, camera access, location access, contacts access, or broad
storage access.

## Android permissions

- **Internet and network state:** live audio and public station information.
- **Foreground media playback and media session controls:** uninterrupted live
  listening and Android system playback controls.
- **Wake lock:** only while Media3 maintains an active live stream.

## Retention and deletion

The last-selected station and local timer state remain on the device until
changed, app data is cleared, or the app is uninstalled. Public station
responses are held only as needed for the active listener experience.
Station-side logs and retention are controlled by the relevant station or
network operator.

This is pre-release software. Do not include personal information,
credentials, or network captures in feedback.

A native Android player that makes it simple to move between five very different stations without losing your place.

Listen without losing the thread

Playback that stays with you.

Choose a station and keep listening through the moments that happen around a song: locking your phone, changing an audio device, or moving through the rest of the app.

  • Clear, responsive play and pause controls
  • Now-playing information and station artwork
  • Queue and History where the station provides them
  • A sleep timer for the end of the night

Follow the music

Discover what is playing next.

See the song in front of you and, when a station shares it, the music around it. The Player keeps unavailable details honestly unavailable instead of guessing.

  • Current track details and station artwork
  • Upcoming and recent tracks when supported
  • Library search for stations that offer it
  • Favorites that stay organized your way

The station, together

Conversation with context.

Where a station supports it, Chat stays in the listening experience with clear station context and native controls.

  • Station-specific conversation
  • Clear message composition
  • Community tools where available

Your station, your way

Features that respect the station.

Each station has its own character. The Player only offers the interactions a station supports, so every feature feels intentional rather than generic.

☻

Chat

Read and take part in supported station conversations with clear controls and station-by-station context.

Song requests

Search and request music where a station makes that possible, with a deliberate confirmation before anything is sent.

⚙

Your preferences

Choose how the Player starts and manage supported station accounts without making every station feel the same.

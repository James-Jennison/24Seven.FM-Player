# TV companion pairing protocol v1 (provisional)

Status: draft 2026-11-02. The Owner settled on 2026-11-02: LAN-only transport and a foreground-only phone. Participants settled by the Owner 2026-11-02: all five stations, only the account signed in on the phone, each request approved on the phone. Still provisional: the `trackRef` shape (see `docs/tv-companion-pairing-design.md`). The mobile repository holds the single copy of this document; the TV side implements it. Nothing is approved or implemented.

## Transport

- TV is the TLS server on the LAN, using a per-pairing key. The phone is the client and pins the SHA-256 of the TV's public key taken from the QR. No other trust path; plain HTTP is never used.
- Frames: 4-byte big-endian length, then a UTF-8 JSON object. Maximum frame 16 KiB; a larger or malformed frame closes the connection.
- Every frame has `v` (protocol version, 1), `type`, and, where it concerns a request, `op` (operation ID, a random 128-bit value generated by the TV, hex). Unknown `type` is ignored unless `v` differs, which closes the connection with `error: unsupported_version`.
- The TV listens only while its pairing or requests screen is in the foreground. The phone connects only while the Player is foreground.

## QR payload (TV to phone)

`v`, `name` (TV name), `host`, `port`, `secret` (128-bit one-time, hex), `exp` (TV clock, UTC seconds; the TV enforces it), `kh` (SHA-256 of the TV public key, hex). Fallback shown beside the QR: a 6-digit code plus the TV IP, which resolves to the same pending pairing on the TV.

## Frames

| type | direction | fields | meaning |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `pair` | phone to TV | `proof`, `phone` (label), `account` (label), `station` | `proof` = HMAC-SHA256(secret, TV key hash); the secret itself is not sent. Account is a phone-supplied display label only, never an email or token. |
| `pair_ok` | TV to phone | `session` | Pairing established; secret is burned. |
| `pair_refused` | TV to phone | `reason`: `expired`, `bad_proof`, `already_paired` | Connection closes. |
| `propose` | TV to phone | `op`, `station`, `trackRef`, `title`, `artist`, `album` | TV asks the phone to request this track. `trackRef` is opaque to the TV. |
| `awaiting` | phone to TV | `op` | Phone is showing its own confirmation; TV shows "waiting for phone approval". |
| `declined` | phone to TV | `op` | Listener cancelled on the phone, or the safety gate refused. Nothing was submitted. |
| `submitting` | phone to TV | `op` | Sent immediately before the station request. After this frame, loss of connection means the outcome is unknown. |
| `result` | phone to TV | `op`, `code`: `accepted`, `refused`, `uncertain`; `reason` (station text for `refused`) | `uncertain` tells the listener to check the Queue. Never retried. |
| `state_changed` | phone to TV | `station`, `account` | Phone's station or account changed; any pending `op` is invalid and the TV returns to unpaired. |
| `revoke` | either | none | Ends the pairing; both sides discard the session and key. |
| `session` | phone to TV | `station`, `account` (label, at most 64), `cookies` (1 to 16 of `name` (at most 128), `value` (at most 4096), `maxAge` (seconds, `-1` for a session cookie), `secure`, `path`) | Session handoff, sent only after the listener approved "Sign this TV in to <station> as <account>?" on the phone. Only cookies of the signed-in station's own origin. The whole frame must stay within the 16 KiB limit; the phone refuses to send a larger one. Values must never be logged by either side (names and counts only). |
| `session_ok` | TV to phone | `station` | The TV stored the session. |
| `session_refused` | TV to phone | `reason` (at most 300) | The TV did not accept it; the phone shows the reason. |

Session handoff notes: protocol version stays 1 (an older peer ignores the unknown types). Decline sends nothing. The handed-over session lives on the TV after the pairing ends and the phone does not revoke it; signing out of the station on the phone does not reach the TV, and the approval text says so. The phone reads the cookies through a domain-level exporter, never from a view model or controller.
| `ping` / `pong` | either | none | Keep-alive every 15 s while connected; two missed means disconnected. |

## Outcome rules the TV must apply

- No `awaiting` or `submitting` seen and connection lost or timed out: show **phone unreachable, nothing was submitted**.
- `submitting` seen but no `result`: show **unknown, check the Queue**; never infer success and never resend.
- `result.code = accepted` only on the phone's explicit confirmation from the station.
- A second `propose` while an `op` is pending is rejected by the phone with `declined`. A repeated `op` the phone already finished returns the same final frame (idempotent).

## Phone-side rules (mobile implements)

- The phone never fetches or posts a URL supplied by the TV. It resolves `trackRef` against its own station request code, verifies it belongs to the paired station, and re-shows the exact track to the listener.
- The phone runs the community safety gate (`MainViewModel.confirmSongRequest` path) before `submitting`.
- Idle expiry on an established pairing is owned by the phone (default 30 minutes without a `propose`); the phone then sends `revoke`.
- Credentials, cookies and session data never cross this channel.

## Open items

- ~~trackRef~~ **Settled 2026-11-02:** `trackRef` = `"<albumId>:<songId>"`, split at the last `:`. `albumId` is the station's `asin` value for the album and `songId` is the numeric `songID` from the track's request link, both as `SongRequestPageParser` reads them. The TV never sends request URLs. The phone loads the album page for `albumId` itself, runs `parseAlbum`, finds the track with that `songId`, and requires it to be currently eligible. It shows the listener the title, artist and album it read from the station, not the text the TV sent (the TV's text is only for the TV's own screen). Any pair that does not resolve on the paired station is answered with `declined`. No track number or other field is needed.
- **TV requirement from the Streamer spike:** the per-pairing AndroidKeyStore key must allow `DIGEST_NONE` as well as `SHA256`, or the TLS handshake fails.
- **Typed pairing (settled with the TV review, 2026-11-02):** the order is connect, read the TV key from the live TLS handshake, show the listener a fingerprint on the phone, and only after the listener confirms it send `pair`. The proof must never leave the phone before that confirmation, because an active MITM could otherwise brute-force the six-digit code offline. The fingerprint is the first 64 bits of the key hash as four groups of four hex characters (`2d71 1642 b726 b044`), shown by the TV in large type and compared ignoring case. The TV shows host **and port** under the code.
- **Proof inputs (both paths):** the HMAC key and message are decoded bytes, never hex text. QR: key = the 16 decoded secret bytes, message = the 32 decoded key-hash bytes. Typed: key = the six ASCII digits, message = the 32 decoded key-hash bytes. The TV's three-bad-proofs lockout burns the code. Test vectors (key hash = 32 bytes of `0x11`): QR secret = 16 bytes of `0x00` gives `fbe4b1db0b7ee3c740efa1e88e5a3bd37e9e63d5792752f5064b43555f97b0c9`; typed code `123456` gives `a9b701cbd81460b50517eeec120d8a1f5771fce14aae158b549edec95d60936f`. If the Owner prefers, typed pairing can still be dropped and the QR kept as the only route.
- Optional request messages (only SST supports them): not in v1.
- Key storage on the TV and QR encoder dependency: TV side.

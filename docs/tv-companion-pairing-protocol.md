# TV companion pairing protocol v1 (provisional)

Status: draft 2026-11-02. The Owner settled on 2026-11-02: LAN-only transport and a foreground-only phone. Still provisional: participants (default: all five stations, only the account signed in on the phone, each request approved on the phone) and the `trackRef` shape (see `docs/tv-companion-pairing-design.md`). The mobile repository holds the single copy of this document; the TV side implements it. Nothing is approved or implemented.

## Transport

- TV is the TLS server on the LAN, using a per-pairing key. The phone is the client and pins the SHA-256 of the TV's public key taken from the QR. No other trust path; plain HTTP is never used.
- Frames: 4-byte big-endian length, then a UTF-8 JSON object. Maximum frame 16 KiB; a larger or malformed frame closes the connection.
- Every frame has `v` (protocol version, 1), `type`, and, where it concerns a request, `op` (operation ID, a random 128-bit value generated by the TV, hex). Unknown `type` is ignored unless `v` differs, which closes the connection with `error: unsupported_version`.
- The TV listens only while its pairing or requests screen is in the foreground. The phone connects only while the Player is foreground.

## QR payload (TV to phone)

`v`, `name` (TV name), `host`, `port`, `secret` (128-bit one-time, hex), `exp` (TV clock, UTC seconds; the TV enforces it), `kh` (SHA-256 of the TV public key, hex). Fallback shown beside the QR: a 6-digit code plus the TV IP, which resolves to the same pending pairing on the TV.

## Frames

| type | direction | fields | meaning |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `pair` | phone to TV | `proof`, `phone` (label), `account` (label), `station` | `proof` = HMAC-SHA256(secret, TV key hash); the secret itself is not sent. Account is a phone-supplied display label only, never an email or token. |
| `pair_ok` | TV to phone | `session` | Pairing established; secret is burned. |
| `pair_refused` | TV to phone | `reason`: `expired`, `bad_proof`, `already_paired` | Connection closes. |
| `propose` | TV to phone | `op`, `station`, `trackRef`, `title`, `artist`, `album` | TV asks the phone to request this track. `trackRef` is opaque to the TV. |
| `awaiting` | phone to TV | `op` | Phone is showing its own confirmation; TV shows "waiting for phone approval". |
| `declined` | phone to TV | `op` | Listener cancelled on the phone, or the safety gate refused. Nothing was submitted. |
| `submitting` | phone to TV | `op` | Sent immediately before the station request. After this frame, loss of connection means the outcome is unknown. |
| `result` | phone to TV | `op`, `code`: `accepted`, `refused`, `uncertain`; `reason` (station text for `refused`) | `uncertain` tells the listener to check the Queue. Never retried. |
| `state_changed` | phone to TV | `station`, `account` | Phone's station or account changed; any pending `op` is invalid and the TV returns to unpaired. |
| `revoke` | either | none | Ends the pairing; both sides discard the session and key. |
| `ping` / `pong` | either | none | Keep-alive every 15 s while connected; two missed means disconnected. |

## Outcome rules the TV must apply

- No `awaiting` or `submitting` seen and connection lost or timed out: show **phone unreachable, nothing was submitted**.
- `submitting` seen but no `result`: show **unknown, check the Queue**; never infer success and never resend.
- `result.code = accepted` only on the phone's explicit confirmation from the station.
- A second `propose` while an `op` is pending is rejected by the phone with `declined`. A repeated `op` the phone already finished returns the same final frame (idempotent).

## Phone-side rules (mobile implements)

- The phone never fetches or posts a URL supplied by the TV. It resolves `trackRef` against its own station request code, verifies it belongs to the paired station, and re-shows the exact track to the listener.
- The phone runs the community safety gate (`MainViewModel.confirmSongRequest` path) before `submitting`.
- Idle expiry on an established pairing is owned by the phone (default 30 minutes without a `propose`); the phone then sends `revoke`.
- Credentials, cookies and session data never cross this channel.

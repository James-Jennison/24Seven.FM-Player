## Why

The owner chose the C2 design direction for the public site: a serif headline
over one sans typeface, light and dark modes that follow the visitor's
device, and five station doors on a Home page sized to fit one screen.

## Content changes

- Home, Stations, Features, Platforms, the privacy pages, the tester program
  page, and the 404 page use the new layouts, stylesheet, and script.
- The developer pages under `/dev/`, the Turnstile test page, and its
  endpoint are removed from the public site.
- The tester catalog file stays at `dev/tester-workspace/index.html` because
  the protected tester portal reads it from disk. The site's `.htaccess`
  answers 404 for every `/dev/` address.
- Instrument Serif and Instrument Sans are served from the site with their
  licence texts. Screenshots and station artwork are display-sized copies.

The privacy notice text, the availability statement, and the relationship
wording are unchanged. The four PHP files, the portal stylesheets and scripts,
the tester task data, and the Cast receiver are byte-identical to the previous
release. The test-case blocks the portal extracts from the catalog are
identical to the previous release.

previous deployment's manifest. The artifact was staged as a sibling

# M30 brand, content, and distribution authorization

Date recorded: August 14, 2026

Status: complete — sanitized repository record

## Authorized scope

An authorized 24Seven.FM Network/Station Administrator, acting with authority granted by the 24seven.FM, LLC
Owner/Operator, authorized the 24Seven.FM Player to use the following for StreamingSoundtracks.com, 1980s.FM,
Adagio.FM, Death.FM, and Entranced.FM:

- station names, logos, owned or controlled branding, station metadata, screenshots, and other public station assets;
- public audio streams for normal live playback in the Player; and
- those assets in the native Player, Google Play listing and review materials, and the 24Seven.FM Player Closed Alpha
  testing program.

The authorization is limited to the unofficial, non-commercial Player and ordinary playback. It does not authorize
stream downloading, rebroadcasting, redistribution, or a transfer of ownership. Third-party material, including album
artwork not owned or controlled by the network, remains the property of its respective rights holder; the Player may
only display it as ordinarily supplied through station metadata.

## Authority and evidence handling

24seven.FM, LLC operates the network. The original authorization identifies the network owner/operator and confirms
that the responding administrator may grant this permission on the network's behalf. This repository intentionally
keeps only the sanitized scope and authority record; it contains no personal credentials, private correspondence, or
reviewer accounts.

## Completion boundary

This closes M30's rights gate for the five named stations and the documented Player/Google Play/Closed Alpha uses. It
does not resolve M29's separate Data Safety, retention/deletion, exact-candidate, reviewer-access, or Play Console
completion gates. It also does not authorize any future commerce, stream redistribution, Google Cast, or unreviewed

# First production release — Google Play availability

**Recorded:** September 27, 2026

## Public status

24Seven.FM Player is available on Google Play in the United States.

## Evidence

- The owner stated on September 27, 2026 that the application is in production and that the website still described it
  as pre-production.
- On September 27, 2026, the public Google Play listing for package `com.codeframe78.twentyfourseven.player` returned
  HTTP 200 for the United States storefront and was read without signing in.

## Scope boundary

This record supports only the statement that the application is publicly listed on Google Play in the United States. It
deliberately does not identify a version, version code, artifact hash, source commit, rollout percentage, or
availability in any other country or region. Version-specific and code-content claims remain governed by their own
release records and manifests.

Closed testing is a separate distribution program and remains governed by its own version-specific release records and
availability observations.

# 24Seven.FM Player 0.1.0-alpha05

Version code: 15
Release stage: Closed-test update pending protected signing and Google Play upload

## Change

- Adds native Google Cast sender support while retaining the existing Compose, Media3, and MediaSession playback architecture.
- Casted playback uses the approved custom receiver and hands off local playback only after the receiver reports remote playback.
- The receiver now presents the selected station, live title, artwork, album and artist metadata, and the station-matched landscape palette.
- Now Playing updates remain live while casting, including safe HTML-entity decoding and title normalization for leading “The”.
- Receiver startup preloads its player library. Physical-device timing measured the app/receiver media handoff at roughly 4.4 seconds; the remaining TV wake/input-switch time is device-side.

## Verification

- `:app:compileDebugKotlin` and `:app:testDebugUnitTest` passed from the integrated release branch.
- The Android sender and custom receiver were accepted on the authorized physical Cast device: remote audio, station changes, live artwork, metadata refresh, palette changes, and title normalization were manually confirmed.

## Release boundary

This is a source candidate, not yet a signed or Google Play-delivered build. The owner-held protected signing flow must create and verify the AAB, after which the owner may upload it to the active Alpha track and validate the Play-delivered update.

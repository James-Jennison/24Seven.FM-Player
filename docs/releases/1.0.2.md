# 24Seven.FM Player 1.0.2

**Track:** Production feature update

**Version code:** 20

**Status:** Candidate in development on `codex/station-features`; not yet built for release or submitted to Google Play

**Source tag:** `v1.0.2` (created when the release candidate is cut)

## What's new

### Listening

- A branded launch screen draws the 24Seven.FM glyph while the saved station is restored, then hands off to the Player
  without a logo flash.
- The station is the hero of the Player: its logo and name sit in the top bar, the Play button takes the station's
  colour, and the tab bar follows it.
- Swipe the artwork left or right to change station, or use the "Next station" and "Previous station" accessibility
  actions. The arrow buttons are gone.
- The cover art cross-fades between tracks instead of flashing the station logo.
- Playback status reads plainly: "Tap Play to listen", "Live", "Paused", "Trying the backup stream…",
  "Waiting for network · resumes by itself", "Couldn't play this station · try again".
- A new launcher icon fits every icon mask and has a themed (monochrome) variant.
- A home-screen widget shows the current track with Play/Stop for the selected station.
- A Quick Settings tile starts and stops the selected station from the shade.
- Long-press the launcher icon for a shortcut to each station; the selected station is listed first.
- Before Play is tapped, the Player already shows what is on air.
- The notification permission is asked for on the first Play rather than at launch.

### Station membership

- Sign in to a station from the More tab. The sign-in form is read from the station's own sign-in page, typed text
  survives rotation, and the password field uses the password keyboard.
- Station chat, the request queue, and song requests with the station's own confirmation step.
- Request activity shows the membership tier and rank, the request clock, and the last 50 requests from the stations'
  upgraded Your Requests page.
- Request messages, request activity, add-to-favourites, and album rating are on for all five stations, not only
  StreamingSoundtracks.com.
- Add the current track to favourites and rate its album from the Player; browse an album's tracks from anywhere it is
  shown.
- Read and send private messages on all five stations, with an unread badge in the top bar.
- Member profiles with public favourites, the listening history archive, and station news.
- The Player shows who requested the track, the audience size, and track progress.
- Station chat reads like an IRC client: one `[time] <name> message` line per message in a bundled fixed-width font,
  day rules between dates, your own name highlighted when mentioned, and names in the station's own role colours
  (Proprietor, Administrator, Moderator, Ambassador, Visitor Mod, composer). Tap a line for its safety actions; a
  one-time hint points this out.
- Member profile cards follow the station's own card: avatar with an online dot, role-coloured name, rank insignia,
  flag, location, member-since, forum posts, badges, and Private message, Email and website links. Email and website
  open the station's page or the member's site in a browser tab.
- Private messages open full-screen with quoted replies shown as nested blocks, and the composer is full-screen with a
  review step before sending.
- Favorites, album track lists and station availability notes are tidied: one-line rows with a request status dot, and
  availability times shown in local time.
- Manage your own favourites from the Favorites tab: each row's menu moves the track up or down or removes it (after a
  confirmation), using the station's own favourites controls; the list is read again afterwards.
- Album reviews: an opened album shows the member reviews from its station page, and when the station offers you its
  write-a-review form you can write a title, text and rating in the Player and send it once after a review step.
- Recently added: the station's newest playlist additions, by day, with covers that open the album.
- Members: who is online now, with the listener count, and the station's members list with search and sort by newest,
  name or posts, paged as the station pages it. Names open the profile card.
- Events & birthdays: the station's theme days, composer birthdays and member birthdays, from today onwards.
- Edit profile: change your real name, location, country, occupation, interests, website, signature, about text,
  newsletter choice and hidden online status on the station from the Player. The password and email cannot be
  changed from the Player.

### First run and settings

- First run is one sheet: pick a station and start listening, or skip.
- The More tab is grouped into Account, the selected station, Community, and App, with Privacy as an expandable row
  that keeps the verbatim privacy sentence, the privacy notice, and the open-source licenses.
- Loading lists show a skeleton rather than a spinner.

### Android Auto

- The car shows the track title and the artist separately, with the station on the artist line and the album below.
- The Queue button lists the track on air followed by the station's upcoming tracks, with title, artist, album and
  cover. Requester names and messages are not sent to the car. Queue rows cannot be selected and skip stays off,
  because live radio cannot jump ahead.
- Stations are offered as a grid with their descriptions.

### Reliability

- Station pages are decoded with the character set they declare.
- Restored station cookies are sent in plain form, and a saved station session ends only on clear evidence.
- Chat sending is reliable behind an in-flight read; sent messages are confirmed with collapsed whitespace; a started
  chat or queue read finishes when its observer leaves; the chat monitor turns off when it cannot run in the foreground.
- The playback session is hardened against foreign media controllers, and the sleep timer and station selection stay
  consistent across Cast.
- Cast startup, notification intents, and mention tracking are guarded against early failures.
- Opening the app from a home-screen station shortcut no longer crashes on devices that show the launch screen
  without its icon.
- The "Trying the backup stream…" status follows the stream that is playing rather than its place in the session list.
- Play's release recommendations for 1.0.1 are applied: the app theme no longer sets the deprecated status and
  navigation bar colours (the Player draws edge to edge through `enableEdgeToEdge()`), the release build uses R8 with
  optimized resource shrinking, and the build moves to Android Gradle plugin 9.4.1 with Gradle 9.7.1 and built-in
  Kotlin. androidx.activity 1.13.0 and core-splashscreen 1.2.0 are picked up with it, and lint passes.

### Tablets

- The tablet landscape Player is redesigned with hardened layout boundaries, and the compact landscape fallback keeps
  the essential controls visible at the former expanded minimum.
- Full-screen views (album, terms, private messages, member favourites, played history, the app guide) keep a
  readable column width on wide screens.

## Candidate scope

- Starts from the code-19 `1.0.1` production lineage plus the `codex/station-features` work listed above.
- Adds a widget receiver, a Quick Settings tile service, and dynamic launcher shortcuts; all three drive the existing
  playback controller and station repository and add no new permissions.
- Station behaviour stays behind the existing capability flags; the four member features verified on
  StreamingSoundtracks.com are now enabled on the other four stations as well. Six new flags (favourite management,
  album reviews, recently added, members list, calendar, profile editing) are on for all five stations, whose public
  pages were checked on October 8, 2026. No stream URL changes.
- The Player remains a reader of station-provided playback, metadata, and member pages; it writes nothing the station
  does not already offer its signed-in members through its own forms. The three new writes (a favourites change, an
  album review, a profile edit) each read the station's own form first and post once to the address it names.
- `PRIVACY.md` gains the request-activity sentence, the fuller list of profile-card fields, and a sentence on the
  Email and website links that leave the app, along with the private-message, favourites and album-rating
  disclosures, and new paragraphs on favourites changes, album reviews, the community pages, and Edit profile. The
  in-app notice says the same. The notice published at 24sevenfmplayer.com still describes 1.0.1 and
  is updated when 1.0.2 is released. No analytics, ads, or tracking.
- Bundles DejaVu Sans Mono for station chat; its licence is in the third-party notices.
- Production targeting is widened from the United States to all 178 countries/regions plus rest of world, saved in
  Play Console on October 8, 2026, sent for review ahead of this release, and approved the same day.
- The media session publishes the station's upcoming tracks as a read-only queue for Android Auto and other media
  controllers. It carries track, artist, album and cover only.
